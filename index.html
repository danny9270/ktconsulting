About

Hi, I'm Kelly

My first job in this world was helping refugee families settle into new lives in Washington DC. Seeing what the right support could do for a family stayed with me. It's why I've spent the last 20 years working where good programmes meet the money that makes them possible.

That path took me to Hanoi with Hagar, to Yangon with MAG clearing landmines, and to Phnom Penh with Goutte d'eau and then DanChurchAid, where I led the programme team as Head of Programme. Most recently I've been Resident Program Director for the International Republican Institute in Bangkok, leading democracy and governance programming.

Along the way I've sat on both sides of the table. I've managed the programmes, and I've written the proposals and reports that fund them, for international institutional donors from the US National Endowment for Democracy to Irish Aid. That means I understand donor compliance, budgets and partner management from the inside, not just the writing. I started my own consultancy so smaller teams can have that kind of support without hiring it full time. I'm practical and down to earth, and I want your proposal to sound like you, not like a template.
